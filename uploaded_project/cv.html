About Me

The Story Behind The Code

Where Logic Meets Creativity

I'm a curious soul who finds equal joy in training machine learning models and perfecting classical dance mudras. Currently pursuing my B.Tech in AI & Data Science at Shiv Nadar University Chennai (CGPA: 8.54), I believe the best innovations happen when technical expertise meets artistic thinking.

My journey is about building bold ideas—from AI-powered educational tools to hospital management systems—while staying rooted in the arts that fuel my creativity.
